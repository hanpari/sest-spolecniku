# Meet SIXBOUND

## Xavier

*professional troublemaker*

He sabotages corporations for a living, and they hate him for it. His greatest enemy, however, is insomnia. He was told that entering the mysterious Game may cure his desease only to discover the treatment is far worse than the disease.

> “It seems the Game fascinates you, doctor.”
“Immensely,” the doctor said with relief. “Believe me, it’s such a revolutionary endeavour that we can’t even imagine its full reach. Truly, don’t underestimate anything,” he repeated firmly.

## Lydia

*princess of a small corporation*

She has no idea how she ended up engaged to Xavier, but he is her only chance to hold her citizen status.

> Among the documents lay a familiar box with the GC logo. Xavier snatched it up quickly and handed it to her, hidden in his palm. *Another Player?* he thought. *This doesn’t look like coincidence at all.*
“Careful,” he warned.
With a suspicious — though not entirely hostile — look, Lydia tucked the box into her pocket. “You know what this is?” she whispered.

## Medard

*family man*

He is doomed to die from a controlled viral infection unless he joins forces with Xavier.

>“If you’re scared,” Xavier said, “keep your distance. No need to feel guilty. And you, future doctor, don’t lecture him.”
“I wanted Mr. Medard to feel safer,” Svetlana shrugged.
“Sure,” Xavier replied. “Without vaccination he’ll die within a few years. How safe does he feel?”

## Gabrielle

*orphan*

She is cold, precise, and unsettling, but she tries her best to act like a loving daughter.

>“You understand correctly,” Svetlana said seriously. “Our father suffers from prejudices, just like Mr. Medard. Though not in all areas,” she added with a hint of indignation. “In a way he has something in common with you. He also can’t stop thinking about her.”
*Her?*
“We’re talking about Gabrielle?”

## Svetlana

*school goddess*

She is dazzling, brilliant, and just a little unbearable.

>“But I think you’re basically a good person,” she noted. “You won’t hurt me. Just like you didn’t hurt that silver woman,” she added with a remarkable hint of disapproval. “Why did you let her go?”
“We’re talking about Xantippa Miller now?” he asked. “You have a remarkable habit of jumping from topic to topic.” He let the threats drop — threatening Svetlana as if she were a street thug felt wrong.

## Grigori

*Svetlana's twin brother*

He will confirm that yes, Svetlana is absolutely unbearable.

> “Svetlana is an adult,” Grigori said with a crooked smile and clenched fists. He didn’t like what Xavier was implying. “It’s none of my business whom she meets or how.”
*Another one playing the villain,* Xavier thought. He noticed how Svetlana flinched when her brother didn’t defend her more.

**Six of them are Players.**

And each of them carries a **second self** — a personality from another World.

## What to expect?

The contrast of two world. One is a dystopian future where citizens are divided into several tiers. One is a fantasy-like Game that is growing like cancer, slowly incorporating itself into the reality.

Plenty of characters and interactions. Fights. Humor.
